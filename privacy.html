Privacy policy

Pen Click Morse · Last updated 3 November 2026

Pen Click Morse turns the sound of a pen clicking into Morse code text. It was built so that nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

Microphone

The app uses your microphone only while you have pressed Listen or are running Teach it your pen. The sound is analysed on your phone, as it arrives, to find clicks. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Pressing Stop turns the microphone off.

What is stored

Your settings (click threshold, timing, vibration) and whether you have seen the setup screen are saved on your phone so they're there next time. Your decoded messages are not saved. You can clear everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

What is shared

Nothing is sent to us. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. When you use Send to a contact, your message is put inside a link and sent through the app you choose (for example Messages or email); it never passes through our servers. If you use Copy or Share, the text goes only where you choose to send it.

Network use

The app downloads its own files when first opened and checks for updates afterwards. It loads nothing from other websites, and works offline once installed.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
