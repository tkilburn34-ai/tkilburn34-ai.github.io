## App name (max 30 characters)
Pen Click Morse

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Click a pen, get text. A Morse code decoder that listens for pen clicks.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Turn any clicky pen into a Morse code key. Pen Click Morse listens through your phone's microphone and types out what you click, letter by letter.

HOW IT WORKS
• One click = dot
• Two quick clicks = dash
• Pause = next letter
• Longer pause = space
• Six clicks = erase the last letter

TEACH IT YOUR PEN
Every pen sounds different. Click yours a few times and the app tunes itself to its sound, your double-click speed, and the noise in the room. It even learns to ignore the softer second click many pens make when you let go.

IGNORES WHAT ISN'T A CLICK
The detector checks every sound for the sharp, short snap of a pen. Talking, music, table knocks and bumps are filtered out, so you can use it in a normal room.

SEND TO A FRIEND
Type a message and your phone plays it as pen clicks. Hold it next to a friend's phone running Pen Click Morse and the message appears on their screen. Add vibration or screen flashes to feel or see the code as it plays.

PRACTICE MODE
Learn Morse code with your pen. The app shows a letter and you send it. Hide the pattern once you know it and build a streak.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Live "click tape" showing every click as it's heard
• Full Morse chart: letters, numbers and punctuation
• Copy or share your decoded message
• Tap pad for when you don't have a pen handy
• Vibrates on each click
• Works offline
• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sound is analysed on your phone and never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Great for passing secret notes between two phones, learning Morse code, escape-room puzzles, scouts, ham radio beginners, and anyone who can't stop clicking their pen.
